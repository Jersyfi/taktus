You ask Taktus for something by writing to it where you already work: in your organisation's
chat, or in a comment in the system where your team keeps its tasks. Which places Taktus listens
in is set up by the people who administer it.

## Before the first time: your account

Taktus acts only for people it knows. Every person has a **Taktus account**, which the people who
administer Taktus create. Your account in the chat is not yet your Taktus account. You connect the
two once:

1. In your Taktus account, you create a **link code**. It is a short code that works once, for
   30 minutes.
2. You write the code in the chat, from your own chat account.

Taktus then knows that this chat account is yours. Creating the code proves that you hold the
Taktus account. Writing it proves that you hold the chat account. A name or an address that
looks like yours connects nothing.

Today a link code is created through Taktus's interface for programs. Ask the people who
administer Taktus to help you with it.

## When Taktus does not know you yet

If you write to Taktus before your accounts are connected, Taktus does nothing with your
message. It answers you in the same place and tells you how to connect your account. It keeps
nothing of what you wrote.

## What happens with what you ask

Taktus turns what you ask into work it can carry out, step by step. Some steps it does itself.
Some it hands to a program or to a person. Where a step needs a person's decision, the work
waits for that person. The page *When Taktus asks you to decide* says how that works.

Everything Taktus does is written down, step by step, in a record called the **ledger**. A
change made to the ledger afterwards would be noticed. So it can always be said what happened,
and when.
