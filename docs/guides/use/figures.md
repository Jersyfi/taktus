Taktus counts what its processes do: runs, steps, time, money. You read those figures in the web
app. No figure is about a named person, except on pages that are yours alone.

## Figures

The page *Figures* shows each view your roles hold, or that someone gave you, with the figures
your roles may read. *As text* writes each figure out in words. Every figure can also be taken
away as a file, in the formats the page links to.

## Reports

The page *Reports* lists the reports your roles receive: who reads each, when it comes and in
which channel. *Compose it now* shows a report's lines at once, without waiting for its time.

## Value

The page *Value* has three parts.

- *What to do*: proposals, computed each time you open the page. Nothing changes until a person
  acts on one.
- *Without Taktus, a role or a supplier*: how far the organisation could run without each of
  them, the processes that cannot run without Taktus, and the suppliers whose removal would
  break a process.
- *The transformation*: each unit's figures, and the work that could still be handed to Taktus.
  Each entry there is an expectation, not a result.

## Your own page

The page *Yours* is yours alone. It shows two things.

- **What Taktus took off you.** For each process that does work your roles did by hand: how
  many runs finished, how long a case took by hand, and the hours regained. Each process links
  to its **takeover instructions**: how a person does the work without Taktus. You can set goals,
  in hours a week, and see which you reached. It is shown as time and tasks, never as a score.
- **How long you take to decide.** Others see such times only added up per role or department.

Nobody else sees this page unless you share it. Under *Your settings*, name the roles you share
it with. Leave the field empty to share it with nobody. A share you withdraw ends at once.

## The coach

The page *Coach* notices requests you make again and again, from your own requests only. It
tells you alone, as a hint: something you could hand to Taktus.

- Switch the coach on or off. Off, it reads none of your requests.
- Choose how many hints a week you receive, or none.
- Answer a hint with *It is automated*, naming the process that now does the work, or with
  *Not for me*. A hint you dismissed does not come back.

The page also says how many of your accepted hints still run.
