A **plan** describes work before it runs: its goal, its steps, the date it is due, how much
Taktus may do on its own, and an estimate of each step. You work a plan out with Taktus in the
web app, on the page *Plan*. Nothing runs until you commission it.

## Starting a plan

Open *Plan*. Taktus asks what you would like to automate. Write it in one sentence, such as
"Send the weekly report to the team every Monday", and choose *Start*. Your plans are listed
below, under *Your plans*, to open again later.

## Two ways to fill it in

A plan can be filled in two ways. Both change the same plan, so switching loses nothing and
nothing is asked twice. The buttons *Guided* and *Expert* switch between them.

- **Guided** asks one question at a time, for the first part the plan still lacks. Each term is
  explained where it first appears. Where a ready-made process fits, it is offered as an answer.
- **Expert** shows every field at once, to set any of them directly.

Under *Every setting*, each setting is shown with its value and why it has that value. Under
*Estimates*, each step's estimate names what it is based on. An estimate without a basis is
marked as a guess. *Still missing* names what the plan lacks.

## How much Taktus may do on its own

A plan names an **autonomy level** and the reason for it. The guided way offers two:

- **Level 1**: Taktus proposes each step, and a person carries it out.
- **Level 2**: Taktus carries out each step once a person confirmed it.

Higher levels are set by the people who may set a process's level.

## Versions and commissioning

Every change makes a new version of the plan. **Commissioning** means you say: run it as
planned. Choose *Commission version N*. Taktus refuses a plan that lacks a part, and names the
part. It also refuses one with a step that cannot be estimated, before anything runs. A changed
plan needs commissioning again.
