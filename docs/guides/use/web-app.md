The web app is where you see what Taktus does and act on it in a browser.

## Signing in

The people who administer Taktus give you two things: the web app's address and your **account
key**. An account key is a secret that begins with `tka_`. Keep it like a password. Open the
address, paste the key and choose *Sign in*. The sign-in lasts twelve hours in every tab, or
until you choose *Sign out*.

may read on it.
