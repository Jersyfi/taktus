# Status of `0.7.0` — second domain

Part of `docs/status.md`: how far `0.7.0` has got, checked against the code and the tests.
What the milestone must reach is in `docs/roadmap.md`. Kept current by every pull request that
changes its state.

**A person's step waits for its role** (#331, UC-4.20 `verified`, ADR-0167 §1 and §2). A
`human` step of the organisation is performed by a person of its role: after the anchors it
waits at every level, its block on `wait.human` with the cause `awaiting_role` and the step's
role, until a person reports it performed — it hands on no result of Taktus's — or rejects it,
and the run halts with cause `declined`. Its work is empty or names the `task`. A fallback to a
person is addressed to the fallback's role. Any identity that may answer the run still answers
it; a right per role is UC-7.3's.

**A role's profile** (#344, UC-15.7 `verified`, ADR-0167 §3 to §5). `GET /roles`,
`GET /roles/{role}` and `GET /roles/{role}/description` derive from the active versions, the
value streams, the anchors, the open decision requests, the runs and the blocks what the
processes need of a role: the streams it is responsible for and serves, the steps it performs and
takes over with their expertise, what it decides and supervises, its load now and if Taktus
stopped, what waits at it and its open requests. Stored nowhere. A figure that persons' work
produced is withheld below two holders; a holder sees their own beside it. No field says whether
a person is needed. The export is a draft position description. **Not to be used before the
labour-law review that DEC-0029 names has covered it** (co-determination, §87(1) no. 6 BetrVG);
every profile and every export says so. No page in the web app yet.

**Autonomy per action class in production use** (UC-7.1, ADR-0170). A tenant's risk class may
carry a level: a ceiling for every step of every process that uses one of its actions, taken on
when a run is created, so that a blueprint's bundle stays the same for every tenant. B-10.03 is
the first process in production use with a level per action: its booking at level 2 inside a
level-3 process, counted per action class (ADR-0110). Migration 0095.

**BPMN in as a plan draft, out as an export** (#345, UC-4.23 `verified`, ADR-0168). A BPMN 2.0 model is read, defused — no document type, no entity, nothing it refers to — into one plan draft per process: every task a proposed step with its dependencies, the role its lane suggests and the method kinds its kind of task suggests, no method chosen, and every question named; a performer is never imported and a loop is dropped with a note. A draft is never a version: `complete` turns the filled draft into a bundle that passes registration's checks, or names every open question. A version exports as BPMN with its gateways, a lane per role, Taktus's extension and a diagram, and every bundle of the repository round-trips its graph, roles and gateways. `taktusctl bpmn draft`, `complete` and `export`, without an instance. The web app's plan dialogue does not read a draft yet.

**Connectors for documents and accounting** (UC-15.8 `verified`, ADR-0169). The documents
connector reads an inbox folder over WebDAV and each invoice in it as the business terms of
EN 16931, from UBL 2.1 or CII D16B, a declared entity refused unparsed. The accounting connector
reads orders, proposes bookings and submits released payments through a generic interface, both
writes `marked`. Both pass the conformance suite against fakes. Neither has run against a real
inbox or accounting system: that needs the tenant's credentials, `DOCUMENTS_TOKEN` and
`ACCOUNTING_TOKEN`.

**Departments share the runners fairly and hold their quotas** (#343, ADR-0166, UC-7.11 `built`).
A run and its job carry the organisational unit its command was given in, the department below
the tenant. The queue's claim shares the places between a tenant's units: unit B's run starts
within two poll intervals behind unit A's two hundred. A tenant sets per unit, with `taktusctl
units`, how many runs hold a place at once and how much money its runs commit per month; both
hold across runners claiming at once, and each refusal is booked as a block (`limit.quota`,
`limit.budget`). A quota is never set on a person. Migration 0092.

**One ledger chain per run** (#341, ADR-0164). Each run's entries form a chain of their own; the tenant's chain holds what references no run, the admissions under a cap on open work, and an anchor once a minute: the Merkle root of the heads of the run chains written since the last. Writers of different runs do not wait for each other: with eight writer processes in one tenant, 1 414 commits per second against 171 with one (`tests/adapters/persistence/test_ledger_throughput.py`, lane `timing`). Reading positions rise with gaps, and a reader that keeps its place reads up to the stable head. Every chain and every anchor verify, a rewrite of a run's chain is found against its anchor, and a ledger written before continues on the tenant's chain unchanged. The ledger is partitioned by month (migration `0091`), which names itself the oldest build it serves. Archiving an old month is not built.

**Package C builds `0.7.0` whole** (DEC-0222), on the branch `concept/package-c`.

**Taktus on more than one node** (#342, ADR-0165). Artifact bytes go to the directory, as
before, or with `TAKTUS_OBJECT_STORE=s3` to a bucket of an S3-compatible store, signed by
signature version 4 on the HTTP client the control plane already has: no library is added.
Both adapters pass one suite (`tests/adapters/objectstore/`), the S3-compatible one against an
independent server of the interface. The chart's profile `ha` renders at least two replicas,
a disruption budget and anti-affinity per role and no volume shared by roles; it refuses the
filesystem store and the chart's own single database. `helm template` of it is checked by
`tests/governance/test_chart.py`. Not yet run on a cluster of several nodes; the external
security review of `1.0.0` is to cover the profile.

**The second domain: invoice to payment** (#402, UC-04, ADR-0169). B-10.03 runs as a bundle:
an invoice read, its order read, every amount computed again by EN 16931's calculation rules in
decimal and matched against the order by the rule `invoice`, `exact`; a booking proposed after a
person's confirmation; the payment submitted only after a person's release at the legal anchor,
with the rule's amount. Proven end to end against fakes. Not built: the daily collection of the
inbox, the classification of an invoice that names no order, and a run against the tenant's real
systems. **Not to be used before the legal-anchor catalogue is reviewed for the tenant's
jurisdiction by a tax adviser** (DEC-0029).
