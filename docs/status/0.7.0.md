**One ledger chain per run** (#341, ADR-0164). Each run's entries form a chain of their own; the tenant's chain holds what references no run, the admissions under a cap on open work, and an anchor once a minute: the Merkle root of the heads of the run chains written since the last. Writers of different runs do not wait for each other: with eight writer processes in one tenant, 1 414 commits per second against 171 with one (`tests/adapters/persistence/test_ledger_throughput.py`, lane `timing`). Reading positions rise with gaps, and a reader that keeps its place reads up to the stable head. Every chain and every anchor verify, a rewrite of a run's chain is found against its anchor, and a ledger written before continues on the tenant's chain unchanged. The ledger is partitioned by month (migration `0091`), which names itself the oldest build it serves. Archiving an old month is not built.
