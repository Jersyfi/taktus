# Status of `1.0.0` — contracts, licence, opening

Part of `docs/status.md`: how far `1.0.0` has got, checked against the code and the tests.
What the milestone must reach is in `docs/roadmap.md`. Kept current by every pull request that
changes its state.

**A breaking change of a published contract fails** (#396, ADR-0171, NTC-0255). `make
gate-compat`, in CI's lane `contracts`, compares every schema and contract OpenAPI document with
the last release tag and classifies each difference, compatible or breaking, from the consumer's
side. A breaking change fails unless it is made as a new major version under a new `$id`.
`tests/tools/test_contract_compat.py` shows both outcomes against a repository with a release
tag. The repository has no release tag yet, only candidates of `v0.2.0`, so nothing is published:
the lane reports a change against the pull request's base and passes. It begins to fail
breaking changes after the first release. ADR-0019 rule 2 is amended: a released schema changes
only compatibly until the freeze. `make gate-contracts` holds the freeze record
`contracts/FREEZE.json` to its digests once the owner writes it (M3.5). It does not exist: the
contracts are not frozen.

**A catalogue of signed adapters and bundles** (#348, UC-14.6 `verified`, ADR-0175). An index
by contract `catalogue/v1` names images and bundles by digest, each signed with Ed25519 over its
own content and stating the tier *reference* or *community*; *verified* is never stated and is
derived from the instance's own conformance and removal records. `taktusctl catalogue` lists the
indexes `TAKTUS_CATALOGUE_DIRECTORY` holds, installs only an entry signed by a key
`TAKTUS_CATALOGUE_KEYS` trusts for its tier — an image as an adapter identifier, printed by
digest for the deployment to pull; a bundle as a proposal, a community one at level 2 or below —
and records it with its origin as `catalogue.installed`; `sign` and `key` are the author's. For
an adapter installed from it, a half taken before the installation does not count, so a
community adapter is refused at level 3 until it is verified here. Migration 0096. Not yet: the
project's own reference index, a release artefact signed with the project's key when a release
is cut; and the community tier of the project's catalogue, which opens with the licence
(DEC-0044) and the contributor agreement.

**A configuration change records its undoing before it is made** (release 1.0, UC-1.3
`built`, ADR-0278). A commissioned plan's changes in a target system are made one at a time:
what undoing each requires is recorded first, every change is an `egress.write`, a deviation
halts with the options and nothing is substituted, a change whose undoing cannot be stated is
handed to a person, and an operation the target cannot recognise a repeat of is never sent twice.
Migration 0107. Not yet: the instance carrying a configuration out on its own, and a person's
own credential per target.

**A connector call is admitted by one rule** (release 1.0, UC-1.4 `verified`, UC-1.6,
ADR-0294). Before every connector call the engine asks one predicate: the catalogue's share of
the connector with circles, and the structure's placement of it (`usable_by`) for the run's
process. A call either source refuses never reaches the connector and is recorded `frame.refused`,
`outside_circle`. A process moved to another unit uses what its new unit may from its next
call on. Not yet: models, agents and skills placed in a unit are held by their share only.

**A domain is worked out as a plan, and its commissioning makes the owned domain** (release 1.0,
UC-15.1 `built`, UC-15.5, ADR-0289). Starting an instance opens a plan draft; the instance's
questions — a tool per capability, the owner, the jurisdiction — are parts the draft lacks, and
every answer is a revision. Commissioning it instantiates the governance domain with its one
owner, its jurisdiction and the plan's processes, then proposes each at level 2 or below.
`taktusctl domain jurisdiction` and `due` are new. Not yet: the removal test with a shipped
blueprint's accounting or customer system.

**A domain's duties go to its owner, and its catalogue is in the conformity evidence** (release
1.0, UC-15.5 `verified`, ADR-0280). A domain's escalations, raise proposals and due takeover
test are addressed to its owner and shown to that person alone (`taktusctl domain duties`);
`taktusctl domain evidence` generates the conformity evidence naming the legal anchors in force
per domain; `taktusctl domain` instantiates domains and names owners. Proven in
`tests/components/governance/test_domain_duties.py` and `tests/integration/test_tenant_domains_command.py`.

**A failure's escalation reaches a person with its situation package** (release 1.0, UC-4.5
`verified`, ADR-0252). Every `run.escalated` is delivered once, with five parts written from the
run's record — what happened, what was tried, what is affected, the options with their risks,
the documentation and access the failed step's instructions name — as a report in the tenant's
owner-facing channel to the role the process names, or recorded only. A part the record cannot
fill stays empty, and the package says which it lacks. A bundle's `escalation` names the role,
the path and the time to react; an escalation unanswered past it is recorded once as
`escalation.overdue`. Proven in `tests/composition/test_escalations.py`; that a running step is
never aborted to escalate, in `tests/components/run/test_engine.py`.

**A foreign agent as a worker** (#349, UC-14.5 `verified`, ADR-0172). The worker contract has a
binding to the open agent-to-agent protocol (A2A 0.3, JSON-RPC), not a second contract:
`workers/a2a/` speaks the contract to the control plane and the protocol to one agent, which
needs to know nothing of Taktus. Its operator names the capability, the location and the
estimate; a step per status change of the agent's task, its seconds as `compute_seconds`, a
steering message sent into the task — which answers an agent that asks for input. It passes the
suite, faults included, against a reference agent; withdrawn, the removal test says *changed*.
No image and no real agent yet: that needs an agent a tenant chose, its address and its
credential `A2A_AGENT_TOKEN`.

**A guided and an expert mode reach the same plan** (release 1.0, UC-13.1 `verified`, UC-10.3,
ADR-0238, ADR-0279). The guided mode asks one question at a time for the first part a plan draft lacks,
explaining each term where it first appears and offering level 1 or 2; the expert mode sets any
field at once. Both revise the one draft, so switching mode loses nothing and asks nothing
twice, and every setting is shown with its value and why. The web app's `#/plans` is the guided
start: it asks what to automate, offers the templates and switches mode with one button. Not
yet: the questions in the chat; the trial with three people who are not technical (UC-10.3).

**A level per class of request** (DEC-0228, UC-03 B-3.03, UC-7.1, UC-7.5, ADR-0215). A
statement names the classes one step's result names, a level for each, a default, and the
limits a class rises and falls by. Every step depending on that step runs at no higher a level
than its class, and a decision on it counts for the class: a class falls one level at its count
of corrections within its window, and a raise of it is proposed on its numbers, never applied.
B-3.02 version 3 of `software-operations` classifies every message into five classes at level
2, with UC-03's numbers as its limits; a tenant releases one class by a version at level 3 with
a person's approval. Proven in `tests/governance/test_classes.py` and
`tests/composition/test_software_operations_engine.py`.

**A model serves real runs only after a trial found the quality held** (release 1.0, UC-8.2,
UC-8.9, ADR-0249). A trial of a purpose is the validation run of every process that asks it: per
process and model it says whether the quality held, each step's score by the process's rules
beside the score its active version had, within the step's tolerance. In a real run a step its
process evaluates on cases reaches only a model whose newest settled validation held; every
other member is passed over as `not_evaluated` in the route. A rehearsal is not held to it. A
model no trial included is offered one (`introduced`). Proven in
`tests/composition/test_model_validation.py`.

**A passive security check of the tenant's own web application** (DEC-0228, UC-03 B-7.09,
ADR-0234). A check of the browser worker that names `security: true` reports whether the page
came over TLS, which protecting headers are missing, which headers name the server's software,
which cookies lack `Secure` or `HttpOnly`, and which of ten well-known paths answers with what
its name promises; nothing is tried on the application, and no value of a header, a cookie or
an exposed path is reported. B-7.09 runs it on the pages given within the frame's allowed hosts
and files a page with a finding once, at level 2. Its active half — test users, identifiers
counted up, admin routes — is not built: what it may try is a person's decision per
application. Proven in `tests/workers/test_browser_worker.py` and
`tests/composition/test_software_operations_remainders.py`.

**A person's stop is followed by an incident and a situation package** (release 1.0, UC-7.2
`verified`, ADR-0280, NTC-0271). Once a stop a person requested holds, an incident `stop.held`
names the stop, the runs it halted and the on-call role (`operator` by default, `taktusctl
incident configure --on-call`), and carries a situation package built by fixed sentences
(`taktusctl incident package`). Proven in `tests/components/governance/test_stop_incident.py`.

**A plan is worked out in versions and commissioned as its own act** (release 1.0, UC-1.2
`verified`, ADR-0237, ADR-0279). A plan draft names its goal, its steps, the date it is due, the autonomy
level with its reason and an estimate per step that names its basis; one without a basis reads
as a guess. Every change is a new version. Commissioning takes one version into a plan
`<draft>@v<version>` and records `plan.commissioned` with who commissioned it; a draft lacking a
part is refused naming it, and one with a step that cannot be estimated is refused before
anything runs. The engine runs only a commissioned plan. Migration 0101. The web app's `#/plans`
and `/plans` present it. Not yet: the chat's presentation; the dialogue's callers fill the
draft.

**A plan that describes a process makes it through the describe process** (release 1.0,
UC-12.3 `verified`, ADR-0290). A draft opened with `describes` results in a process version;
commissioning it hands its goal to the process `TAKTUS_DESCRIBE_PROCESS` names, whose run keeps a
proposal. The proposal is rehearsed and commissioned by a person. Without a describe process,
such a draft is refused before anything is written. An onboarding assistant goes from words to
a registered version that runs, is counted and runs at its stated level, without code.

**A process is built from a description** (release 1.0, UC-4.1 `verified`, ADR-0251). Words
for a process the tenant does not have — on `POST /processes/{id}/proposals` or in the owner's
chat — start the process `TAKTUS_DESCRIBE_PROCESS` names; the repository's is S-08. Its bundle
becomes a proposal against no version only through registration's validation; a method guessed
against its class leaves none. A rehearsal's outward step says under `would_send` what it would
have sent. Commissioning is the person's act. Proven in
`tests/composition/test_process_from_a_description.py`.

**A process leaves Taktus as one package of plain files** (release 1.0, UC-8.9, ADR-0285).
`taktusctl process export <process> --to <file>.zip` writes a version as JSON and as BPMN, each
prompt, its takeover instructions, what an instance provides for it, the skills its steps use in
their open format, a README and a manifest of digests; the same version gives the same bytes,
and the export is recorded as `process.exported`. Proven in
`tests/composition/test_process_package.py`.

**A project is a unit of the structure, and a private one is its owner's alone** (release 1.0,
UC-1.6 `built`, ADR-0276). A project is created alone, for one's team, a project team, or the
company by a role holding `project.company`; it refers to tickets and documents and copies
nothing. A private project is in no other context and in no figure above it, and only its owner
moves anything across its edge or shares it. `taktusctl structure project`, `/projects`,
`#/structure`. Not yet: the engine refusing a connector the project does not share.

**A residency rule narrowed per process** (release 1.0, UC-11.1, ADR-0258). The tenant's rule
names a location per process, never wider than its own; for a run, the narrowest of the
tenant's, the data class's and the process's holds at the choice of a model and of a worker.
`taktusctl residency set --process payroll=own`. Proven in
`tests/components/governance/test_residency.py` and `tests/components/run/test_worker_residency.py`.

**A result against the moving median, and its window found again** (release 1.0, UC-4.10
`building`, UC-4.11 `verified`, ADR-0271). A check of kind `distribution` marks a result suspect
that lies outside a band around the median of the step's earlier results, after it counted; a
check names its method, a statistic or a rule; a result no check judged is `result.unchecked`.
A result defect's window starts after the shape of what the step read changed, or, with no
change, at the earliest result its check marks when run again. Proven in
`tests/exactness/test_distribution.py` and `tests/governance/test_result_defects.py`.

**A run answers in the channel its command came from** (release 1.0, UC-1.1 `verified`,
ADR-0239). The scheduler says, once, in the thread a command arrived in, that its run finished,
needs a person, or waits for the person's answer, and records `run.answered`. Ends older than an
hour are answered by nobody, so an upgrade does not answer old runs. Not yet: the run's result in
the answer; a process posts it with a step of its own.

**A run is paused or cancelled through any channel, and "what are you working on?" is answered
from the ledger** (release 1.0, UC-1.5 `verified`, ADR-0236). `status`, `pause <run>` and
`cancel <run>` are read from every message whose sender is placed, on any channel; the answer is
said in its thread at once, and the scheduler says there when a pause or cancellation took
effect at the run's next step boundary. A cancelled run never resumes. Only the identity a run
acts for, the owner, or a role holding `run.steer` may steer it. Migration 0101. Not yet:
resuming from a chat, and the words of a tenant's own phrasebook.

**A session with a project's knowledge** (release 1.0, UC-1.8 `building`, ADR-0277). One
session per participant and project, continued after closing and on every channel; every
statement names its source or says it has none; it acts on nothing and settles in a plan draft or
a named record. `/sessions`, `#/structure`. Migration 0107. Not met: a chat channel reaching it,

**A sourced step declares a check** (#92, UC-4.10, ADR-0233, NTC-0268). A bundle with a
`sourced` step that declares no check does not register, and the finding names the step and the
catalogue. Every `sourced` step of the blueprints declares one: a connector's answer the
members the bundle reads from it, a composed text its fixed words, a model's answer its pattern;
68 bundles carry a new version, P-03 version 25 among them. The shape of the source holds an
object to the members it must carry, in any order. Restating UC-4.10 and UC-4.13 in the
catalogue's rows waits for a pull request of its own (NTC-0268). Proven in
`tests/exactness/test_exactness_statement.py` and `tests/exactness/test_checks.py`.

**A span leaves masked by the organisation's policy** (release 1.0, UC-5.8, ADR-0286). Where
spans are exported over OTLP, the copy that leaves has every attribute named by
`TAKTUS_OTLP_MASKED_ATTRIBUTES` replaced whole, and every e-mail address, bank account number
and telephone number in a text replaced, as `TAKTUS_OTLP_MASKED_KINDS` names them, all three by

`tests/adapters/telemetry/test_masking.py`.

**A step on behalf of a person reads with that person's own credential** (release 1.0,
ADR-0264). A connector step marked `on_behalf` is served with the own credential of the
identity its run acts for, named per identity in `TAKTUS_PERSONAL_CREDENTIALS`; an identity

**A tenant exported and imported elsewhere verifies there** (release 1.0, UC-10.6 `verified`,
ADR-0221, NTC-0265). `taktusctl tenant export` writes every row of every table with the tenant's
name — processes, runs, ledger, provenance, decisions, identities, traces — and the bytes of every
object those rows name, with the newest hash of every chain and the document's digest.
`taktusctl tenant import` writes it into a tenant that holds nothing, then checks it there: the
ledger from its first entry, every chain's newest entry, every run's provenance chain; exit code
1 when a check fails. The administration key reaches both on the HTTP surface. Proven against
two databases: a finished run and a waiting one, entry for entry the same, the waiting run
continued on the second instance. Needs the database: development state in files exports
nothing.

**A tenant's monthly budget across all its runs** (release 1.0, UC-8.5, ADR-0260, migration

**A worker with a browser** (DEC-0228, UC-03 B-7.07 to B-7.09, ADR-0218). `workers/browser/`

**Data-protection requests and the processing evidence** (#657, UC-11.2 `verified`, ADR-0176).
`taktusctl subject prepare` names a kind — access, rectification, erasure, portability — and a
subject, and lists every run that served one of the subject's cases, found through the
provenance records whose scope names it and through the runs' intake, with each step, object
and trace line. Preparing changes nothing. `taktusctl subject confirm --identity-checked`
carries it out for the person who checked the requester's identity, on exactly the holdings

first entry and each run's provenance chain after it; an export is generated and never stored;

never the subject. `taktusctl evidence` prints what is processed, where, by which adapter and
kept how long, generated from the register of what acts, the tenant's declarations and the

`/subjects/requests` and `/evidence`, and the web app's page *Data protection*, which says back
what a confirmation will do and asks for the identity check before it sends it. A blueprint
process prepares a request as a step: B-3.08 recognises it, checks the sender through the
product and prepares it there for a person (ADR-0208).

**Images scanned, signed and pinned** (#347, ADR-0173). `.github/workflows/images.yml` publishes

without, held by `helm template` in `tests/governance/test_chart.py`. **Runs only on `main`**:
the workflow has not run yet; the scanner, the proof and the digest-first push were run by hand
against a local registry. Built fresh on 2026-10-31, the control plane, the three workers and a
connector passed; the backup image passed once its `rclone` and `age` came from their projects'
releases, pinned by SHA-256, instead of the distribution, whose builds carried fixed critical
vulnerabilities (ADR-0174). Not a release process (M3.6).

**Limits per unit of the structure, inherited and watched for harm** (release 1.0, UC-8.5,

a team, or each person of a unit on their own, a limit in money, compute seconds or tokens per day, week or month, for every
process or one. A step is admitted only where its priced reservation fits what remains of every
limit on the run's path; a child never lets a step through its parent, and children are booked
within or over a parent as configured. At the line the run halts for a person or waits for the
next period. Alerts at a share of the amount or of the forecast band, the line reached, an
anomaly by a robust median rule and a limit that held too many runs or too long reach the
limit's role as incidents; the harm report recommends an amount with its cost and benefit, and
Taktus never changes the limit. `units applies` shows what a run or a process needs, used and is
held by; `units consumption` shows what one person spent against a limit held per person to that person
alone. Not built: the policy
"a cheaper model", and the estimated demand shown before a run starts. Proven in
`tests/components/run/test_unit_limits.py` and `tests/components/governance/test_unit_limits.py`.

**One machine's load, measured** (release 1.0, S-04, ADR-0223). `taktusctl load measure`
submits the probe of three rule steps at each level of concurrency and reads each run's latency
from its own record: per level the end states, median, 95th percentile, longest latency and runs

**Point-in-time recovery, recovery targets per profile, and the restore drill** (#346, UC-10.5
`built`, ADR-0174). An operator enables `backup.pointInTime.enabled`: the chart's database
archives every segment of its write-ahead log into the backup's store, and each backup run takes
a base backup beside the dump; off by default, and the small profile backs up without it. Each
profile states its recovery point and recovery time objective in `deploy/k8s/README.md` §2, and
the single server in the guide's page *Restoring*. The drill S-03 (`backup.drill.enabled`,
weekly) restores the newest backup — or to a chosen moment, `drill --to` — into a server of its
own pod, never the instance's database, recomputes every tenant's chain by the ledger's published
rule without Taktus, holds it to the heads taken before, and fails beyond the machine's part of the
recovery time. Proven against a database of the tests' own: a restore to a moment between two
batches ends at the heads noted then, by the drill and by hand; an altered entry and a moment the
archive does not reach fail it. On one machine the compose file's service `backup` backs up
every 24 hours and drills the newest backup every seventh day (`backup.py schedule`, ADR-0222),
so that the single server's recovery point is 24 hours; a drill that fails there is recorded as a
failed run at the step `drill` and reaches the owner as a failed backup does. `make up` pulls
published images where `TAKTUS_IMAGE` names them and builds from the checkout otherwise; none is
published before the first release tag. Not yet: a drill on the installed instance, and on a
cluster a drill told to the owner when it fails — its Job and its report in the store say it.

**The organisation's structure decides what is seen, shared and paid for** (release 1.0, UC-1.4

**Versions and deprecations follow written rules** (release 1.0, ADR-0220). A patch fixes, a

**What a higher limit would have bought, and how to wait less on people** (release 1.0, UC-9.5
