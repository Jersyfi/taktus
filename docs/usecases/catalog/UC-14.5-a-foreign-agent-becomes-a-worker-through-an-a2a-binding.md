# UC-14.5 — A foreign agent becomes a worker through an A2A binding

## 1. What must be achieved

An agent that speaks the open agent-to-agent protocol (A2A), built by anyone and run anywhere,
can serve a step as a worker. It does so behind the worker contract, not beside it: the step
gets the contract's estimate before it starts, the contract's limits while it runs, the
contract's event stream with its step boundaries, a stop at a boundary, and the removal test.
The agent needs to know nothing of Taktus. A process names what the agent does by capability,
never the agent or its product. The organisation keeps its choice of where its data goes, as
for every worker.

## 2. How it is verified

- A reference agent, a small server of the protocol that knows nothing of Taktus, behind the
  binding passes the worker conformance suite: every check the suite runs against a worker
  reached at an endpoint passes, and none is inconclusive. Every fault the binding can be
  started with fails exactly its check.
- The binding estimates before the agent is sent anything. An estimate above the limits is
  rejected before anything reaches the agent. A running total that would cross a limit halts
  the assignment at a step boundary, named.
- A frame that does not allow the agent's host or the binding's capability sends the agent
  nothing, and the refusal is visible in the stream.
- The agent's credential reaches the agent and nothing else: no event, artifact, state or log.
- A message a person sends to the running step reaches the agent at a step boundary, once; an
  agent that asks for input is answered that way.
- The removal test withdraws the binding and finds *changed*, not *broke*: the step moves to
  another worker of the capability.
- The binding declares where the agent stores what it is sent, as its operator states it. A
  binding whose operator states nothing does not start.
- No product name appears in the core, in the binding or in the reference agent.

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not a second worker contract**: the control plane speaks the worker contract to the binding,
and nothing else. **Not Taktus exposed as an agent to other systems**: that is the other
direction, and not required here. **Not the agent's inside**: what the agent calls and reaches
is not held to the step's frame, and a stop pauses the observation, not the agent. **Not money
the agent spends** unless it reports it: the binding measures what it can observe. **Not every
version and transport of the protocol**: one published version over its JSON-RPC binding. **Not
a verified location**: the location is what the operator declares (UC-11.1 §3).

## 4. What it rests on

The worker contract (ADR-0007, UC-14.1); a foreign service behind the contract and a declared
location (ADR-0157, UC-8.15, UC-11.1); the removal test (UC-8.9); steering at a boundary
(ADR-0139). Source: `docs/research/2026-10-12-market-review.md` §2.2.7, DEC-0190, issue #349,
milestone `1.0.0`.

## 5. What is proven so far

Every condition of section 2 against the reference agent: the binding passes the suite, W-12
and W-21 pending as for every worker reached at an endpoint, and each of its faults fails
exactly its check; an estimate above the limit, limits without compute and a frame without the
agent's host send the agent nothing; an agent's request for input is answered by a message
steered into its task; a run hands its step to the agent; withdrawn, the removal test says
*changed* with a second binding as the alternative; a binding without its location or its
capability does not start. Not proven: a real agent, which needs its operator's address and
credential; another version or transport of the protocol.
