# UC-14.6 — A catalogue of signed adapters and bundles, in three tiers

## 1. What must be achieved

An operator finds adapters and process bundles in a catalogue. The catalogue is an index: each
entry names a signed image or a signed bundle, what it serves and who signed it. Taktus reads
the index and runs nothing from it. An entry is in one of three tiers. A *reference* entry is
kept by the Taktus project. A *verified* entry has passed the conformance suite and the removal
test in this instance's own configuration. A *community* entry is signed by its author and is
`experimental`; it runs at autonomy level 2 or below until it is verified here.

An instance installs only an entry whose signature it can verify against a key its operator
trusts for that entry's tier. It records in the ledger what it installed and where it came
from, so that the origin of every installed entry can be read back later.

## 2. How it is verified

- An entry without a signature, with a signature that does not verify, or signed by a key the
  operator does not trust for the entry's tier is refused, with the reason. Nothing is recorded
  as installed, and nothing of the entry reaches the tenant.
- An entry whose bundle does not match the digest it was signed over is refused.
- An installed entry is recorded in the ledger with its tier and the digest of the entry as it
  was verified. The stored entry names the index it came from, its signer's key and the
  artifact's digest.
- An index never makes an entry *verified*. An index that says so is refused. *Verified* is
  derived in the instance, from its own conformance and removal records.
- An adapter installed from a community entry is `experimental`. A step on it runs at level 2
  or below. A conformance pass or a removal test taken before the entry was installed does not
  count for it.
- The same adapter becomes *verified*, and may serve a step at level 3, once the conformance
  suite and the removal test both passed for it in this instance after it was installed.
- A bundle from a community entry is installed as a proposal at autonomy level 2 or below, and
  is refused above. A person decides on the proposal as on any other.
- The catalogue lists every entry with its tier and, for an installed adapter, its tier in this
  instance and what keeps it from *verified*.

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not a store.** Nothing is bought, sold or paid for through the catalogue. **Not a platform
that runs code**: an image is pulled and started by the operator's own deployment, by digest;
the catalogue only says which digest is trusted. **Not the marketplace** of
`docs/vision/beyond-1.0.md`, which stays after `1.0.0`. **Not open to outside contributions
yet**: the mechanism for community entries is built, and the project accepts community entries
only once the licence (DEC-0044) and the contributor agreement are settled. **Not the
publication of the project's reference index**: that index is a release artefact, signed with
the project's key when a release is cut. **Not a judgement of quality**: a signature says who
published an entry, and verification says it conforms here, not that it is good.

## 4. What it rests on

ADR-0009, which moves community adapters to their own repositories from `1.0.0`; ADR-0012, the
licence that is not settled; ADR-0044, the conformance half the instance measures itself;
ADR-0030, the removal test; ADR-0039, which runs a step at level 3 only on a verified adapter.
DEC-0190, feature F8, which accepted the catalogue with tiers and its use case first. Issue
#348, roadmap `1.0.0`.

## 5. What is proven so far

Every condition of section 2. An entry unsigned, signed by a key the operator does not trust,
signed by a community key for the reference tier, changed after it was signed, or whose bundle is
not the one it was signed over is refused with the reason, and nothing is recorded. An installed
image and an installed bundle are recorded with their tier, author, index and evidence in one
transaction with `catalogue.installed`. An index that states *verified* fails the contract and
the model. An adapter installed from a community entry behind an identifier that was verified
reads *experimental* and its step at level 3 halts with cause `maturity`, until both halves pass
again after the installation; then the step runs. A community bundle above level 2 is refused,
one at level 2 becomes a proposal. `taktusctl catalogue list` shows each entry's tier, why it is
refused, and an installed adapter's tier here with what it lacks. Not built: the project's own
reference index and its community tier (section 3).
