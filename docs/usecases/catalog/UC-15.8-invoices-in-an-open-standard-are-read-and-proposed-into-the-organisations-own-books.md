# UC-15.8 — Invoices in an open standard are read, and proposed into the organisation's own books

## 1. What must be achieved

An organisation receives invoices and keeps its books in a system of its own. Taktus reads an
electronic invoice in the open European standard, whichever of its syntaxes it arrived in, and
reads what the organisation ordered from its own accounting system. It hands a booking proposal
and a released payment into that system. It keeps no books of its own.

Neither the document nor the books tie the organisation to a product: the invoice is read by an
open standard, the books are reached through a generic interface, and either can be replaced by
another connector serving the same capability.

## 2. How it is verified

- An invoice of EN 16931 in UBL 2.1 and one in CII D16B are each read into the same set of
  business terms. Every amount is the decimal text the document gave; nothing is rounded or
  computed in the reading.
- A document that declares a document type or an entity is refused before it is parsed. A
  document that is no such invoice, or a name that reaches outside the inbox folder, is refused
  as invalid.
- An order is read from the accounting system with what was received and invoiced against it.
- A booking proposal and a released payment each reach the accounting system once per
  idempotency key: a repeat finds the record and creates nothing. The key does not leave Taktus.
- A malformed amount, date or currency, or a payment of nothing, is refused before the
  accounting system is asked.
- Both connectors pass the connector conformance suite, fault for fault; the accounting
  connector's two writes pass C-05.
- Every capability is named by function, never by product, outside the connector's own
  directory (CLAUDE.md §6).

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not an accounting system.** Taktus proposes; posting, the record's unalterability and the
payment run stay in the organisation's system (`docs/vision/non-goals.md`). **Not every
document.** A scan or a PDF without its structured invoice is not read. **Not the tax adviser's
package.** Preparing filings and closings is the finance blueprint's (UC-15.2), not this case.

## 4. What it rests on

The connector contract v1 and its suite; connectors for a business's systems (ADR-0150); the
documents and accounting connectors (ADR-0169); changing a vendor breaks nothing (UC-8.9); the
finance reference domain (UC-15.2, `blueprints/finance/`). Numbered for the roadmap's `0.7.0`
item "connectors for documents and accounting", the next free number of its area (NTC-0252).

## 5. What is proven so far

Every condition of section 2 is proven by the named tests: a UBL and a CII invoice read in one
shape, amounts as given; a declared entity refused unparsed and a document above 5 MB refused;
a non-invoice and a name outside the folder refused as invalid; an order read; a booking
proposed once per key, its mark derived from the key, which the system never sees; a malformed
amount and a payment of nothing refused before the system is asked; both connectors pass the
suite, the accounting connector's writes C-05. Neither connector has been run against a real
inbox or accounting system yet.
