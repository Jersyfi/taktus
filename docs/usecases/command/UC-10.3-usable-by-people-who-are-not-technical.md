# UC-10.3 — Usable by people who are not technical

## 1. What must be achieved

A person who is not technical gets a first automation working on their own. Taktus starts by
asking what they want to automate, speaks plainly, starts with safe settings, and offers templates
for typical private and business situations. Every department can use Taktus without opening a
ticket with its IT, within the governance the organisation set.

## 2. How it is verified

- A trial with at least three people who are not technical and have never used Taktus: each sets
  up a first automation from the guided start without opening documentation. The trial is recorded
  with every place a person stopped, and each stop is fixed or recorded as a finding.
- A process created through the guided start begins at autonomy level 1 or 2, with its reason
  (ADR-0026). It goes higher only through the raise of UC-7.1.
- The guided path uses no technical term without explaining it where it first appears.
- Templates exist for typical private and business situations; a template is a blueprint or a
  scaffold of the catalogue (UC-15.1, UC-13.2).
- A person with the right to create processes in their unit does so without anyone in another unit
  acting for them (UC-7.3).

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not a simplified product.** The guided start leads into the same system and the same settings;
the two ways of working are UC-13.1. **Not every situation.** Templates cover typical ones and grow.
**Not the installation.** Getting Taktus running is definition `UC-10.1`.

## 4. What it rests on

The guided mode (UC-13.1), of which the guided start is the first use (ADR-0029); the autonomy
statement (ADR-0026) and the raise (UC-7.1); blueprints and scaffolds in the catalogue. Definition
`UC-10.3`. Filed under `command` by ADR-0029. The roadmap names no version; `0.6.0`, with the
catalogue its templates come from, is the session's proposal.
