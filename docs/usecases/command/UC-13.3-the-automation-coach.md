# UC-13.3 — The automation coach

## 1. What must be achieved

Taktus teaches people to notice recurring tasks in their own day and to automate them for good:
through hints from their own use ("you ask this every Monday"), small learning nudges, and the
discovery of processes nobody has written down. Hints are few and to the point, can be switched
off, and lead to automations that last rather than to toys used once. What the coach observes also
feeds the first stage of the skill lifecycle.

## 2. How it is verified

- A hint is drawn from the person's own requests and is shown to that person only.
- The person sets how many hints a week they receive, down to none, and the default is stated where
  it is set. A hint the person dismissed is not shown again for the same pattern.
- Switching the coach off is one setting. From then on the person's requests are no longer
  observed for hints.
- For every hint that was accepted, Taktus records whether the automation it led to still runs
  after a period the tenant configures. The share is shown to the person; anyone else sees it only
  aggregated by role or department.
- What the coach passes to the skill lifecycle describes a procedure of a process, never how a named
  person works. A draft drawn from one person's work belongs to that person (UC-14.2).

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not process discovery.** Bringing unwritten processes into the open is UC-9.4, in
the `value` component; the coach points to it. **Not a learning platform.** No courses, no
scores. **Not proposals for processes in general**, which are UC-4.4.

## 4. What it rests on

Proposals drawn from a person's own work (UC-4.4); the skill lifecycle (UC-14.2); the protective
rule that what concerns a person belongs to that person (ADR-0015, principle 14). Definition
`UC-13.3`. Filed under `command` by ADR-0029. The roadmap names no version; `0.6.0`, with the skill
lifecycle, is the session's proposal.

## 5. What is proven so far

Built by ADR-0240, and shown by the named tests: a weekly or frequent request among a person's
own commands becomes a hint offered to that person alone; the person sets one to seven hints a
week or none, one by default; a dismissed pattern is never offered again; switching the coach off
stops reading requests, and nothing asked while it was off is read later. An accepted hint is
checked after the tenant's period against the ledger's runs of the version it led to; the person
sees their own share, others the share per role. What is passed on is a procedure owned by the
person, naming nobody. A person sees, dismisses and accepts their hints, and sets their coach,
in the web app (`#/coach`, ADR-0279), where the default is stated.
