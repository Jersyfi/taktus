# UC-13.5 — A person's own contribution made visible

## 1. What must be achieved

Taktus shows each person what the relief from monotonous work brings them: which recurring tasks
it took off them, how much time that freed for their actual work, and which contributions of
higher value became possible. The story it tells is consistent: the machine takes the monotony,
people keep the business, the creativity and the decisions.

That view is the person's own. It is never turned into a measure of the person.

## 2. How it is verified

- A person's own view is visible to them alone by default. Showing it to anyone else is their act,
  and they can withdraw it.
- Every other view shows what Taktus took off people only at process level, aggregated: no figure
  in any other view can be traced to one named person. A group so small that it would identify one
  person is not shown.
- The data model has no field that attributes a figure of work or performance to a named person
  outside that person's own view. A test fails when one is added: the rule is enforced in the data
  model, not in a policy (principle 14).
- Time freed is shown as time and as tasks, never as a score, a rank or a comparison between
  people.
- The view links to the takeover instructions of the processes that took work off the person
  (UC-6.3), so that it is visible that people keep the knowledge and the control.

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not people analytics.** No productivity, no utilisation, no comparison of people. **Not
gamification.** Success is told as time regained and the person's own goals reached, not as points
(definition `UC-13.4`). **Not a statement about anyone's job.** The view shows what happened, not
what it means for employment.

## 4. What it rests on

The `reporting` component, where ADR-0029 files enablement's views; the protective rule of
ADR-0015, the same shape for response times; the takeover instructions of UC-6.3. Definition
`UC-13.5`, epic E13 — enablement, which has no component of its own (ADR-0029).

## 5. What is proven so far

The rule of the data model, by the named tests (#94, NTC-0169): no value type of the core and no
table holds a figure beside a field that names a person, except a record of one act without a
total and a read of one's own with the test that proves nobody else reads it; a field added that
breaks it fails the test. The own view (ADR-0245): only the person reads it until they share it
with a role, and withdrawing closes it at once; it shows each process that relieves their roles
with its runs, the time by hand, the time regained and the link to its takeover instructions, as
time and tasks, never a score; elsewhere a figure of relief whose roles by hand one identity
holds is withheld.
