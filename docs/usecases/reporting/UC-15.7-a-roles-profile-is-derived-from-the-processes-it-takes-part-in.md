# UC-15.7 — A role's profile is derived from the processes it takes part in

## 1. What must be achieved

For each role of a tenant's structure, Taktus shows what the processes need of it. The profile
names the value streams the role serves, the steps it performs, what it decides and supervises,
what it must be able to take over, and the expertise all of that needs. It shows the role's load
now and the load it would carry if Taktus stopped, what waits at the role, and the proposals open
for it. The profile can be exported as a draft position description. It is never a file about a
person.

## 2. How it is verified

- The profile of a role lists, from the tenant's active process versions, value streams,
  anchors and decision requests: the streams it is responsible for and the streams it serves;
  each step it performs, with its task and expertise; each step it takes over as a fallback;
  each anchor and each step's question it decides; each check it supervises; the expertise of
  all of these; the waits open at it now; the steps Taktus performs that would come to it if
  Taktus stopped; and the decision requests open for it.
- A time figure, and any figure that the work of persons produced, is withheld where fewer than
  two identities hold the role. The withheld field says why. A reader who holds the role sees
  their own figure beside it. Figures of what Taktus performs are not withheld.
- No representation of a profile, the export included, carries a field that holds a person or
  says whether a person is needed, how many or which one. The profile states that a workforce
  decision stays a strategic anchor.
- The export is a draft position description that says it is a draft and is not to be used
  before the labour-law review DEC-0029 names. It carries no time figure and nothing of the
  reader's own.
- The profile is read from existing records and stores nothing of its own. A process the reader
  may not see contributes nothing. Another tenant sees no profile of this one.

## 3. Where the boundary lies

**Not an HR system.** Nothing is entered, kept or tracked about a person; the profile is read and
exported only. **Not a workforce decision.** Whether a role needs a person, or more of them, is a
strategic anchor and the owner's. **Not the labour-law review itself.** The review that DEC-0029
names is a professional act outside the product; until it has covered the profile, the profile
is not to be used in earnest. **Not a page in the web app.** The profile is required on the
HTTP surface.

## 4. What it rests on

A person's step names a role (UC-4.20, ADR-0146) and a run addresses it there (ADR-0167); the
value streams (UC-15.6, ADR-0159); the withholding of a figure that would be one person's
(ADR-0015, NTC-0168); reporting reads and owns no figure (ADR-0029); anchors keep a workforce
decision with a person (ADR-0008). The owner accepted it as F3 of the product outlook
(DEC-0190, Zenkit idea D of `docs/research/2026-10-12-market-review.md`), and issue #344 carries
it; NTC-0253 records the use case. ADR-0167 §3 to §5 is how it is built. The labour-law review is DEC-0029's.

## 5. What is proven so far

`GET /roles`, `GET /roles/{role}` and `GET /roles/{role}/description` derive the profile from
the records and leave the stores as they were. Each part of section 2 is shown on facts handed to
the pure derivation: the duties, the streams, what the role decides and supervises, the
expertise, the load now and if Taktus stopped, the waits and the open requests; the withheld
figures below two holders beside the holder's own; no field that holds a person or says whether
one is needed; the export as a draft that names the review — by the named tests. Another tenant
sees no profile of this one. **The profile is not to be used before the labour-law review that
DEC-0029 names has covered it.** A step that a person performed after its model's fallback is
counted as Taktus's in the load if Taktus stopped, because its step run keeps the model's method
(ADR-0167, *Where this promise ends*). No page in the web app.
