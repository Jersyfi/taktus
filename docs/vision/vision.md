# Vision

## What Taktus is

Taktus is an operating layer for a business.

A person states what should be achieved. Taktus breaks that into processes, decides for each
step which method is best suited to it, runs it or has it run, measures the result, corrects
within an agreed frame, and reports back — from the operational detail to the strategic figure.

People lead. Taktus carries the recurring work.

At its furthest extent this is a **virtual agent business**: an organisation whose domains —
engineering, sales, purchasing, partner interfaces, accounting, tax — run as orchestrated
process chains. People remain the owners, the responsible parties and the deciders of every
domain. Taktus is the workforce for everything that repeats.

## What it is not, said first

Not another tool landscape. Not another system to maintain beside the ticket system, the
repository and the wiki. Taktus is the layer above what is already there. It replaces no
instrument; it makes them play together.

What Taktus itself produces — assistants, dashboards, documentation, skills — is always a
Taktus process or a Taktus artifact in an open format: exportable, removable, never
compulsory. The rule holds even where Taktus creates something new.

## The idea that separates it

**AI is not a synonym for language models.** That conflation is the mistake this product does
not make.

Eight methods are available to a process step — `rule`, `statistics`, `ml`, `neural`, `llm`,
`worker`, `human`, `wait` — and **four of them are reproducible**. Reproducibility is therefore
not a question of "AI or no AI" but of which method was chosen.

Taktus chooses per step, records why, measures the choice and proposes a change when a cheaper
and more reproducible method would do the same job. The path usually runs one way:

```
language model  →  classical ML or a specialised model  →  rule
   expensive                  cheap                        free
    variable                reproducible                  exact
```

Operating time therefore becomes an asset: an organisation collects the training data for its
own models while it works, and Taktus notices when there is enough of it.

**Nobody has to know that classical ML exists in order to benefit from it.** That is the
difference from a workflow tool with AI nodes, and it is the sentence the positioning rests on.

## Positioning

*The sovereign, AI-native operating layer for processes — for everyone who wants to be forced
neither into a cloud suite nor into a construction kit.*

European at its roots, usable anywhere. Digital sovereignty — self-hosting, own models, own
hardware — and European values — data protection, transparency, human control — are product
features, not a compliance exercise.

## Who it is for

| | |
|---|---|
| **Individuals and families** | small daily automations, a model shared within a family, self-hosting on their own hardware |
| **Small and medium businesses** | transformation without an AI department; an orchestrator that reports to them |
| **Enterprises** | cross-departmental orchestration, a controlling layer, multi-tenancy, governance |
| **Public sector and regulated industries** | sovereignty, air-gapped operation, auditability, EU conformity |

**One core, a smaller footprint.** The enterprise views and controlling functions are
expansions of the same core, not a second product line. No autonomy level and no capability is
reserved for organisational size.

## What must be true for this to work

Three things, and each is measured rather than asserted:

**Dependence on Taktus must be dissolvable.** Every process it runs carries maintained
instructions. The takeover test passes when a person can run the process without Taktus.

**Dependence on any supplier must be dissolvable.** Removing an integration may change quality
or cost, never break a process. The removal test proves it, weekly, as a process Taktus runs
for itself.

**A promise must state where it ends.** Every architecture decision that promises something
names the boundary of that promise. A promise without a stated boundary is read as a guarantee,
and that is where the disappointments come from that a product never recovers from.
